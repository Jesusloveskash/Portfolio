Different industries. Same question.

Research, finance, and product work keep bringing me back to what people actually need.

By Kash Vinayak · First-person portfolio field note

Scientific research taught me to investigate unfamiliar questions. Financial workflows made the importance of accuracy and reliability very concrete. Product work brought the user back into every decision.

The common thread is not a single industry. It is understanding a system, finding the friction inside it, and building something useful for the people working with it.

These are reflections on my experience, not claims about quantified business outcomes.
